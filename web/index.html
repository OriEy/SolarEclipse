Flat Earth Eclipse

A real total or annular eclipse, replayed on the azimuthal-equidistant disc with a small sun circling overhead. The umbra positions are real; the object casting them is reconstructed.

Eclipse

Model

The shadow only fixes the line from the sun to the umbra. The object can sit anywhere on it, so its height is a free choice. The diameter it needs follows from the sun's diameter: it has to cover the sun as seen from the shadow, plus the width of the umbra itself.
